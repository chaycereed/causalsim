# causalsim: Simulation-Ready Causal Data Generating Processes

Construct, simulate, and evaluate causal data generating processes
(DGPs) with known ground truth. Covariate roles (confounder, effect
modifier, noise) and heterogeneous treatment effects are first-class
concepts, and estimator performance is summarised with bias, RMSE,
confidence-interval coverage, and power. Results are returned as plain
data frames for further analysis or plotting.
